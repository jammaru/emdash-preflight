# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include being considerate, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Report incidents through the repository's private contact channel.
